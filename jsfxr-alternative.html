A jsfxr-style generator with batch variation export

jsfxr, bfxr, and ChipTone are excellent retro sound-effect tools. Retro SFX Lab is a newer browser alternative focused on a fast game-jam workflow: tune a sound once, then export a small set of usable variations with license and reproducibility metadata included.

Why try it?

Batch export

Generate five small variations as a ZIP in one click. The ZIP includes the WAV files, a README, and settings.json with the exact parameters used.

Shape the transient

Attack time and decay-shape controls make it easier to create soft UI clicks, sharp hits, short laser zaps, and longer power-up tails.

Local and simple

No signup, upload, server processing, or install. Sounds are synthesized in your browser and exported as standard WAV files.

What you can export

  • single WAV files for immediate engine import;
  • five randomized but parameter-controlled variations in one ZIP;
  • settings.json documenting waveform, frequency sweep, duration, noise, bit depth, attack, decay, and volume;
  • shareable URLs that encode the current sound parameters.
