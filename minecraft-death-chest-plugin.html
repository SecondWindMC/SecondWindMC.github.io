Restart-safe Minecraft death chest plugin

GraveGuard places player items and XP into a protected grave when they die. Graves survive server restarts, protect loot from other players, and guide the owner back with a compass.

What makes it different

  • Active graves are serialized and restored after restarts or reloads.
  • Items are stored in the grave record, so oversized inventories do not spill or vanish.
  • Exact XP mode preserves the player's level progress as experience points.
  • Only the owner and bypass-permission staff can open a protected grave.
  • Graves are explosion-protected and include owner/time holograms.
  • The return compass is delivered after respawn and points to the grave.

Built for real servers

Crash-safe: a restart no longer deletes every active death chest.
Recovery-safe: grave contents are returned through the same inventory, armor, offhand, and overflow rules validated in automated tests.
Configurable: protection time, despawn time, holograms, XP mode, search height, compass, messages, and prefix are editable in config.yml.

Install

1. Download GraveGuard 1.1.0 for Paper 1.21.x.
2. Put the jar in plugins/.
3. Restart the server.
4. Edit plugins/GraveGuard/config.yml.
5. Run /graveguard reload.

GraveGuard was validated on Paper 1.21.11 with death, restart, intruder-protection, owner-recovery, item, and XP checks.
