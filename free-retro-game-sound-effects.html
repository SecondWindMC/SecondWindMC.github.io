Free retro SFX FAQ

Are these sound effects really free?

Yes. The 12-file sample is free to download and use. You do not need to create an account or provide an email address.

Can I use them in a commercial game?

Yes. The sample sounds are released under CC0, so commercial games, videos, streams, prototypes, jams, and apps are allowed.

Do I need to credit you?

No. Attribution is not required. If you want to credit the pack, “Circuit UI SFX Free Sample by SecondWindMC” is enough.
