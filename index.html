How a fight plays out

You're downed. Lethal damage drops you to a crawl — a red boss bar counts down your bleed-out. No fighting, no items, no teleporting out.

A teammate channels a revive. They hold SNEAK beside you while a progress bar fills. Taking damage or moving breaks the channel.

…or an enemy finishes you. The same channel lets the other side execute. Bleed-out and executions are real deaths — your lifesteal plugin treats them normally.
