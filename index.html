How a fight plays out

Three beats. Every engagement gains stakes it never had before.

01

You go down

Lethal damage drops you to a crawl — a boss bar counts down your bleed-out. No fighting, no items, no teleporting out.

02

They hold SNEAK beside you while a progress bar fills. Taking damage or moving breaks the channel.

…or you get finished
